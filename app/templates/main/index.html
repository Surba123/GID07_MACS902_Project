Three security layers

Identity, integrity, and confidentiality

01 / PKI

Certificates establish who holds the signing key.

A protected Root and Issuing CA bind an RSA public key to a reviewed local identity. Validation checks the full chain, purpose, lifetime, and current revocation list.

View certificate workflow →
02 / SIGN

Digital signatures expose any byte-level change.

RSA-PSS and SHA-256 protect a canonical detached manifest while trust remains a separate, explicit decision.

03 / ENCRYPT

Recipient-only sharing keeps content private.

Ephemeral X25519, HKDF-SHA-256, and AES-256-GCM ensure only the intended account can decrypt.

Academic scope

This local prototype demonstrates real cryptographic flows. It does not provide trusted timestamps, production account recovery, multi-recipient encryption, or HSM-backed key custody.
